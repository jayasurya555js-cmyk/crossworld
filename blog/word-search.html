Five Tips to Find Hidden Words Faster

1. Scan systematically

Search one row or column at a time instead of looking randomly around the grid.

2. Look for unusual letters

Distinctive letters can make possible word locations easier to spot.

3. Check different directions

Words may appear in multiple orientations depending on the puzzle rules.

4. Focus on longer words

Longer words can be easier to recognize because they have more distinctive patterns.

5. Avoid rushing

Careful observation is often more effective than random guessing.
