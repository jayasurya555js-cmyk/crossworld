A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Choose a puzzle that fits your mood

Crossworld brings together short, browser-based games that are easy to start and simple to share. You can work through crossword clues, scan a letter grid for themed words, or make a puzzle from words you choose. There is no account to create, and the games run directly in your browser.

How the crossword works

Read the Across and Down clues, select a numbered square, and enter one letter in each cell. Where two answers cross, they share a letter, so a correct answer can help you solve another clue. Use Check to see whether your filled letters are right, or use one of the limited hints when you get stuck. The rotating crossword collection includes compact 5×5, 6×6, 7×7, and 8×8 boards.

Look for a theme in the word search

Each word search has eight words from a familiar theme such as animals, fruit, the ocean, space, sports, school, weather, or kitchen items. Find the listed words in the 12×12 grid by dragging across a word or tapping its first and last letters. Choose New Puzzle to switch to another theme; Reset starts the current theme again.

Make a puzzle of your own

In the crossword creator, add up to eight words and write a clue for each. Words must fit the 8×8 board and share letters so they can cross. Generate the grid, check its preview, then copy or share the link for someone else to solve. The puzzle is carried in that link rather than saved to an account, so only share it with people you want to see its words and clues.

Race to the finish in Snakes & Ladders

Play against the computer, take turns on one device, or invite friends to an online room. Choose a two-, three-, or four-player game, roll the die, and race toward square 100: ladders move you up and snakes send you back. A six earns another roll, and you need the exact roll to finish. Choose a game mode.

Choose a mode in Slice

Swipe across fruit and vegetables to score, but avoid the bombs. Classic is the score-chasing mode; Zen gives you 60 seconds without bombs, while Frenzy is a 45-second round with double points. The Slice game also explains its power-ups and unlockable blades.

Solve clues under pressure in Neon Word Range

Each round gives you a crossword-style clue and a short answer to build. Aim and click or tap to shoot the needed letters, avoid glitch bombs, and keep an eye on the 90-second timer, accuracy, and combo score. Open Neon Word Range to play.

Chase clues in Clue Chaser

Read each rapid-fire crossword clue and intercept the matching answer as it races down a neon vector grid. Choose the correct lane before the answer breaches the grid, then keep chasing clues through the arcade challenge. Play Clue Chaser free online.

Flip gravity in Gravity Shift

Tap or click to reverse gravity and guide a glowing orb between floor and ceiling hazards. Dodge spikes, lasers, and moving barriers, reach checkpoints, and earn bonuses for close calls. Play Gravity Shift, a free physics arcade game for quick reflex rounds.
