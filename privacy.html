Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Crossworld ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Crossworld website and its browser-based games at cross-world.in. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the site, use a game, or follow a shared puzzle link.

No account required

You can play the games without creating an account. We do not ask you to provide a name or email address to use the basic puzzle features. Crossword puzzles you create are encoded in the shareable URL rather than stored in a Crossworld account. Anyone with that URL may be able to view the puzzle, so share it only with people you choose.

Information processed when you use the site

Our hosting and service providers may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, and request time. Google Analytics may also process page views, interaction events, approximate location inferred from network information, and device or browser information. We use these reports to understand site usage and improve the games.

Some games run primarily in your browser. If you choose an online multiplayer feature, the player name you enter and game or room messages may be transmitted to the multiplayer service to operate the session. Do not use a real name or include sensitive personal information in a player name or game message.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, mobile or browser identifiers, and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown, detect invalid activity, and, where permitted by your settings and applicable law, personalize ads using information about this and other sites or apps. These providers may process information such as IP address, browser or device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and general location.

Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services in the Google partner-sites policy and review Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and privacy choices

Google Analytics and advertising services may use cookies or similar storage technologies. You can manage Google's ad personalization in My Ad Center and review Google's ad settings. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings and use available industry opt-out tools, such as the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page. These controls may be browser- or device-specific, and opting out of personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads.

If your browser or local law provides privacy signals such as Global Privacy Control, you may enable them in your browser. The availability and effect of such signals can depend on the service and applicable requirements. Blocking cookies may affect analytics or advertising but should not prevent the core browser games from working.

How long information is kept

We do not maintain user accounts or a user profile database for the basic games. Information handled by Google Analytics, Google AdSense, hosting providers, or multiplayer services is subject to those providers' retention practices and settings. Multiplayer room information is used to operate active sessions and may be cleared when a room closes or expires.

International processing

Google and other service providers may process information on servers in countries different from the one where you live. Their processing is governed by their own privacy terms and safeguards.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request about information we control, contact us using the details below. For information processed by Google, use Google's privacy and ad settings linked above.

Children's privacy

Crossworld is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request. Advertising and data processing for children must follow applicable laws and platform requirements.

Third-party websites and services

This site may link to third-party websites and uses third-party services, including Google Analytics and Google AdSense. We do not control their websites or privacy practices. Review their policies before using those services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, practices, or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was most recently updated.
