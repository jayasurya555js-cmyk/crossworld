Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Crossword Challenge ("we", "us", "this site") is a free crossword puzzle game. This page explains what data is collected when you use the site and how it's used.

No account required

You don't need to sign up, log in, or give us your name, email, or any personal details to play, create, or share a crossword. Puzzles you create are encoded directly into the shareable link itself — we don't store them on a server.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used — for example, which pages are visited, how many puzzles are solved, and roughly how long a session lasts. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information, which may include your approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser type, and the pages and actions you interact with on this site. This data is aggregated and is not used by us to identify you personally.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure ad performance. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and adjust your ad personalization settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies

Cookies set by Google Analytics and Google AdSense are the only cookies used on this site. We don't set our own tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work, though analytics and ad personalization will be affected.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
