How to play Slice

Fruits and vegetables fly up from the bottom. Swipe your finger or mouse across them to slice them and score points. Slice 3 or more in one swipe for a combo bonus. Missing a fruit or veggie costs a life, and slicing a bomb ends the game. The longer you play, the faster it gets.

After about 20 seconds, glowing power-ups start to appear. Slice them to use them: ❄️ Freeze stops everything for 3 seconds, ⭐ Double points for 8 seconds, ❤️ Extra life, 🎉 Fruit shower, 🐌 Slow motion, 🧲 Magnet and 💨 Bomb clear. Look out for the golden apple (10 points) and the giant watermelon (3 slices). Pick a mode before you start: Classic, Zen (60s, no bombs) or Frenzy (45s of fruit showers, all points x2). Reach score milestones to unlock new blades.
